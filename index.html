Who are you?

If an alien asked you this question, would you know how to answer? My work explores identity and the things that make us human. Through crochet, I build small totem plushies that celebrate the family and friends I hold close. My hope is that self awareness and celebration of our differences can build a stronger sense of belonging in our everyday communities.

My first identity was artist. Since the moment I learned to draw a horse, paint with watercolor, or perform in the school play, I had always been drawn to art and self expression. This passion has since materialized in knitted scarves, music, acrylic paintings, and now crochet.

My second identity is engineer. As a recent graduate in mechanical engineering, I’m proud to be working in HVAC to help communities save energy and lower costs. The fight for a healthier planet is a global effort, and it’s our choice to protect the world for not only us but the generations that follow.

Engineering Projects

SEP 2025 - MAY 2026 • CSUF Research & Capstone

Solid-State Hydrogen-Powered Speedboat

Researched and engineered a zero-emission watercraft powered by solid-state hydrogen storage and high-performance fuel cell propulsion. Developed custom rapid-prototyped hull components, integrated power distribution, and incorporated sensor telemetry for real-time vessel monitoring.

Electronics & Telemetry Bambu H2S Prototyping Project Management
Sep 2024 - Apr 2025 • Titan Aero

RC Aircraft Delivery System

Contributed to the Controls sub-team for Titan Aero (SAE Aero Design competition). Designed a radio-controlled aircraft capable of transporting a 40-lb payload. Handled telemetry configurations, wire soldering, Solidworks FEA stress analysis, and laser cutting to assemble the prototype in a record 4 months.

Solidworks FEA Electronics & Telemetry Manufacturing Prototyping
Nov 2024 - Dec 2024 • CSUF

Smith Machine Design

Acted as Project Lead for a team of 3 to design an original Smith Machine assembly capable of handling a 4,718 lbs static load. Developed a comprehensive technical drawing package adhering strictly to ASME Y14.3/14.5 standards, with shear stress analysis applied to 1045 steel safety mechanisms under a safety factor of 10.

SolidWorks ASME Standards Stress Analysis Project Management
