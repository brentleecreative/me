Aug 2025 - Personal Project

Soil Monitor

A moisture sensor system designed using an Arduino Uno microcontroller and C++ programming. It accurately detects soil water levels, mapping sensor resistance data to a 0-100% moisture percentage and outputting it to a real-time OLED display in order to prevent over- or under-watering of house plants.

Arduino Uno C++ Sensors Breadboard Wiring
Sep 2024 - Apr 2025 • Titan Aero

RC Aircraft Delivery System

Contributed to the Controls sub-team for Titan Aero (SAE Aero Design competition). We designed a radio-controlled aircraft capable of transporting a 40-lb payload. Involved in telemetry configurations, wire soldering, Solidworks FEA analysis, and operating laser cutters to complete the prototype in a record 4 months.

Solidworks FEA Electronics & Telemetry Manufacturing
Nov 2024 - Dec 2024 • CSUF

Smith Machine Design

Acted as Project Lead for a team of 3 to design an original Smith Machine assembly capable of handling a 4718 lbs static load. Developed a full drawing package adhering to ASME Y14.3/14.5 standards, analyzing shear stress on safety mechanisms with a factor of safety of 10 applied to 1045 steel.

SolidWorks ASME Standards Stress Analysis Project Mgmt
